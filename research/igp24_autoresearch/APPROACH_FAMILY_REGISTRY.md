## Portfolio rules

- Preserve independence in early research rounds. Give an agent only the
  artifacts and constraints needed for its assigned mathematical family.
- Share the currently favored mechanism only with its execution and audit
  group. Other research families receive no favored-proof prompt.
- One memory-heavy Sage/GAP worker maximum. Light audits may run in parallel.
- A stalled family is blocked until a proposal adds a materially new
  construction, invariant, dispatcher, or obstruction.
- Re-rank families by verified golds per source trial and wall-clock hour, not
  elegance or forecast alone.
