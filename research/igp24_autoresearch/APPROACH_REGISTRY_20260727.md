- Give each family only the shared live boundary and its own construction data;
  do not disclose another family's favored mechanism during the first round.

  preference for current team-count 0 and 1 pairs.

  undiscovered/unheld group-signature frontier. A team-count 1 raid is
  secondary unless it unlocks a materially larger team-count 0 closure wave.
- Require exact construction provenance, local certification, current target,
  baseline, ownership, coefficient-novelty, receipt, and duplicate gates.
- Permit at most one memory-heavy Sage/GAP worker at a time.
- Block a family after its declared pilot if it has no fresh exact hit or if
  its remaining attainable score is immaterial to the 38.239614-point gap.
- Cross-pollinate only after root records the measured strengths, failures, and
  obstructions of the independent first-round pilots.
