# Attribution and reuse

The MIT license applies to Team Dirac’s original code and accompanying documentation in this repository. Existing third-party notices, if present in a file, continue to apply to that material. We do not claim authorship of external mathematical software, group catalogs, official competition data, or imported seed data.

The coefficient lists and recorded mathematical results are provided for inspection and reuse. Please preserve their source and verification context when redistributing them. Citation of this release helps distinguish the local archived records from later changes to the competition’s data.

- **Competition:** [SAIR Foundation IGP24](https://competition.sair.foundation/competitions/igp24/overview), with its organizers and official verifier. The leaderboard and placement fields are factual exports attributed to their public source URLs.
- **LMFDB:** [The L-functions and Modular Forms Database](https://www.lmfdb.org/). The curated 8×3 seed notes identify the imported field [8.6.65106259.1](https://www.lmfdb.org/NumberField/8.6.65106259.1) and the construction derived from it. Consult LMFDB’s own terms for its material.
- **Software dependencies:** [SageMath](https://www.sagemath.org/), [GAP](https://www.gap-system.org/), [PARI/GP](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/), [Magma](https://magma.maths.usyd.edu.au/magma/), and SymPy. These tools and their catalogs are not redistributed here as team-owned software. Magma requires its own access/license.
- **Model assistance:** Aishwarya reports using GPT‑5.6 Pro for strategic work and Codex for implementation and continued experiment loops. Per-message model metadata is unavailable for the retrieved chat. Historical outputs may contain mistakes; their inclusion documents the process.

- **Article:** Written with AI assistance from the team’s conversations and records. Its conceptual illustrations were generated with Image Gen. Prompts are included. The empirical graphs use the supplied data and are separate from the illustrations.

No statement here claims that every accepted polynomial is an original or exclusive realization, or that every code idea originated with the team. The archive records what the team generated, submitted, and used during its effort.
