1. Emergency verdict
1.   Stop optimizing verified-polynomial count. Optimize three-day retained score.
Between August 6 and August 12, Dirac gained 70 scoreable pairs but lost 150.282409 points. The score-per-pair
fell from 0.029148 to 0.022635, a 22.34% deterioration. The central failure is therefore portfolio decay and
revaluation, not a shortage of accepted polynomials.
2.   The best immediate new attack is a common-quadratic-resolvent 8x3 fiber product.
This is not the exhausted linearly disjoint octic-cubic compositum. Force an octic normal closure and an S3-cubic
normal closure to intersect in the same quadratic field. The resulting degree-24 action is the subdirect product H
x_{C2} S3, not H x S3. It has exact group-theoretic certification, useful signature control, and a cheap abstract
GAP atlas before any expensive arithmetic.
                                       H x_{C2} S3 = {(h,s) in H x S3 : chi(h) = sign(s)}

3.   Run one independent cheap reserve in parallel: exceptional elliptic 7-division fields.
The 7-division polynomial has degree 24 in the x-coordinates. Instead of random curves, enumerate transitive
mod-7 image classes, identify their exact 24T actions on nonzero torsion points modulo sign, and join that finite
atlas to the live target table. This is fundamentally unrelated to Kummer extensions, twists, subset resolvents,
and tower volume.

 Deadline constraint
 The published deadline is August 15, 2026 at 00:00 AoE, equivalent to August 15 at 7:00 AM CDT. As of 12:48 PM CDT
 on August 12, roughly 66 hours 12 minutes remained. The requested seven-day campaign is impossible under the
 published schedule unless the competition is extended.

Ranked causal diagnosis
1. Competitor reproduction is almost certainly the largest term
For a pair with k credited teams and Dirac discriminant D, the score is:
                                                    2^(1-k) * log(D0) / log(D)

Every new team normally halves the pair's base value. A collection of 300 Dirac solos becoming two-team pairs
would lose approximately 150 points. Alternatively, 100 solos going from k=1 to k=3 would also lose
approximately 75 points twice over. These are illustrations, not claims about the exact holder transitions.

2. Pair-level exact/mixed discriminant transitions may have amplified the loss
For a non-baseline pair, if any relevant nfdisc computation fails or times out, the entire pair is scored with the
mixed fallback. This can change the score of every team on that pair, including historical rows. New competitor
submissions can therefore change both k and the discriminant source.
Operational implication: submitting another hard-to-evaluate polynomial for an already owned pair is not
automatically harmless. Even when it does not change ownership, it can waste evaluation capacity and may
alter the pair-level discriminant pathway.

3. The new waves generated mostly low-retention coverage
The totally-real wave concentrated 119 rows into six labels. The Kummer wave concentrated 75 rows into only
15 pairs. Those are family-correlation signatures, not target-conditioned breadth.
The unit-twist wave was internally broad, but the total net ownership increase shows that many outcomes were
already owned, became immediate collisions, or landed in already crowded strata. The supplied bundle does not
contain the pair-by-pair pre-wave ownership table needed to distinguish those cases.

4. Internal duplication explains wasted throughput, not the full score loss
Dirac contributes only one team count to a pair regardless of how many polynomials it submits, and within one
submission only the first verified row for a pair is considered. Internal duplicates therefore cannot directly

6. Attack 1: common-quadratic-resolvent 8x3 fiber products
Let A/Q be an octic field with normal closure N_A, transitive group H, and an index-two character chi:H -> C2. Let
k be the corresponding quadratic subfield of N_A. In the first implementation, use the sign character, so:
                                               k = Q(sqrt(sqf Disc(A)))

Construct a non-Galois cubic field B/Q with Galois closure N_B, group S3, and quadratic resolvent exactly k. Cubic
fields with a prescribed quadratic resolvent have an explicit class-field/Kummer parametrization and can be
enumerated by conductor.
When N_A intersection N_B = k, the normal closure of AB has group:
                                                    G = H x_{C2} S3

A particularly clean proof gate is 3 not dividing |H|. Since N_B/k is cyclic cubic, any intersection larger than k
would force a factor of three into |H|. Therefore the intersection is exactly k.
The compositum AB has degree 24 because [A:Q]=8 and [B:Q]=3 are coprime. The fiber product acts transitively
on 8x3 points and has two useful crossing block systems:
   eight blocks of size three, with quotient H;
   three blocks of size eight, with quotient S3.
The group has order 3|H|, rather than 6|H| for the direct product.

Signature control
Let r_A be the number of real roots of the octic source.
   If k is real, the cubic is totally real and r(AB)=3r_A.
   If k is imaginary, the cubic has one real root and r(AB)=r_A.
The exact involution classes in H x_{C2} S3 determine which values actually occur. The abstract atlas computes
this before constructing any polynomial.

Exact certification
A candidate certificate contains:
4. the exact octic group H;
5. the exact index-two character and quadratic field k;
6. proof that the cubic is S3 with quadratic resolvent k;
7. proof that N_A intersection N_B = k;
8. the exact product-action 24T identification;
9. exact root count;
10. irreducibility and degree 24 of the reduced compositum polynomial;
11. exact nfdisc.
This is an exact containment proof, not a Frobenius classifier. A full Magma GaloisProof can audit one
representative per resulting action.

Discriminant strategy
   low-discriminant octic sources;
   the smallest possible quadratic resolvent;
   cubic conductors supported on small primes;
   ramification outside k disjoint from the octic source;
   polredabs or equivalent field reduction before generating the monic submission polynomial.

7. Attack 2: cyclic cubic ray-class extensions of octic fields
Take an exact octic field K and a ray-class quotient of exact order three. Construct a cyclic cubic class field L/K
with [L:K]=3, giving [L:Q]=24.
This is not a direct 8x3 compositum. In the normal closure, conjugates of the order-three character generate an
F3[H]-module. The kernel may be C3^d, with its rank and dependency structure determined by the orbit of the
ray-class character. This targets odd-module block kernels absent from Dirac's predominantly 2-primary
Kummer campaign.

Structure and signatures
The degree-24 action has eight blocks of size three. The quotient on the blocks is the normal-closure group of K. A
cyclic odd-degree extension cannot complexify a real place. Consequently:
                                                           r(L) = 3r(K)

The possible signatures are 0, 6, 12, 18, and 24.

Discriminant
                                                          d_{L/K} = f^2
                                                   |D_L| = |D_K|^3 * Norm(f)^2

Choose low-discriminant octics and the smallest split primes whose ray groups contain useful order-three
quotients.

     Enumerate 24T groups with degree-eight quotient matching a candidate octic group;
     Enumerate 24T groups with elementary abelian 3-kernel;
     Enumerate 24T groups with the exact involution fixed-point count 3r(K).

8. Attack 3: narrow cyclic quartic extensions over sextic bases
Choose a degree-six field K and a ray-class quotient containing a genuine order-four character. Construct L/K
cyclic of degree 4, with [L:Q]=24.
This directly targets exponent-four kernels rather than elementary C2^d Kummer kernels. The order-four
quotient and its 2-cocycle must be retained; an extension that collapses to C2 x C2 is rejected as an old lane.

Signature control
At each real place of K, a cyclic quartic Galois extension contributes either four real embeddings or zero real
embeddings if that place complexifies. The list of infinite places in the ray modulus therefore gives signature
chambers with:
                                                    r in {0,4,8,12,16,20,24}

subject to the real places of the chosen sextic.

Discriminant
                                        d_{L/K} = f(chi) f(chi^2) f(chi^3) = f_4^2 f_2

9. Attack 4: S3-Galois sextics over quartic bases
Let K/Q be quartic. Choose a quadratic extension M=K(sqrt(d)) with a prescribed sign pattern at the real places of
K. Let tau be the nontrivial automorphism of M/K. Find an order-three ray-class character psi satisfying:
                                                        tau psi = psi^(-1)

The resulting cyclic cubic class field E/M is stable under tau, and Gal(E/K) is isomorphic to S3. Since [E:K]=6,
[E:Q]=24.
This creates nonabelian sextic block kernels. It is not the pure-cubic fiber from F4 that forced at most one real
root over each real outer embedding.

Signatures
     If M splits into two real places, each supplies three real embeddings and the contribution is six.
     If M becomes complex, the contribution is zero.
For a totally real quartic base, r(E) lies in {0,6,12,18,24}.

Exact target class
The degree-24 action has four blocks of size six. Its local block action is S3, and the global normal closure is
determined by the conjugate anti-invariant character module and extension cocycle.

Gate
    Pilot
    Run eight fields from four distinct quartic bases. Scale after two exact live outcomes and at least 30 live structural
    targets.



    Kill
    The anti-invariant 3-primary spaces are uniformly zero, every action is already in the direct-product catalog, or
    class-field equation construction exceeds the deadline budget.




10. Attack 5: D4 and Q8 extensions over cubic bases
Let K/Q be cubic. Construct a Galois extension E/K of degree eight with group D4 or Q8. Then E is an absolute
degree-24 field.
For D4, begin with a biquadratic field M=K(sqrt(a),sqrt(b)). A standard construction solves a norm equation:
                                                  Norm_{K(sqrt(a))/K}(u) = b

and adjoins the required square root over M. The resulting closure is checked for exact D4. For Q8, solve the
corresponding central embedding problem; Witt-type criteria translate solvability into equivalence of ternary
quadratic forms, and explicit solutions can be recovered from the equivalence matrix.

Signature control
Every real place of the cubic contributes either eight or zero real embeddings.
     For a totally real cubic: r=0,8,16,24.

three blocks of size eight and a nonabelian block kernel.

11. Attack 6: exceptional elliptic 7-division x-coordinate fields
For an elliptic curve E/Q, the seventh division polynomial psi_7(x) has degree (7^2-1)/2 = 24 and its roots are the
x-coordinates of nonzero 7-torsion points modulo P ~ -P.
If the mod-7 image is G <= GL_2(F7), the exact degree-24 permutation action is on:
                                                       (F7^2 - {0}) / {+/- 1}

For the full image, the faithful action is through GL_2(F7)/{+/-I}. Complex conjugation has eigenvalues 1,-1. It
fixes three projective sign-pairs in each eigenspace, giving exactly r=6.

Why exceptional images matter
Random curves with surjective mod-7 image all land in the same group/signature cell. That pair is likely already
crowded. The only rational campaign is:
12. enumerate realizable exceptional image classes;
13. retain only those transitive on the 24 sign-pairs;
14. identify their exact 24T labels;
15. join to the live table;
16. choose one low-conductor curve per image class.
This is the first attack architecture not suggested in the prompt or attachments.

    Gate
    Kill immediately if the finite image-action atlas contains no live valuable pairs. Continue only if at least one exact
    live pair appears among the first five distinct image classes. Never run multiple ordinary full-image curves.




12. Attack 7: elliptic 5-torsion point fields
The nonzero vectors of E[5] ~= F5^2 form a 24-element set. A full mod-5 image acts transitively on them.
The x-division polynomial alone has degree 12 because it identifies P and -P. Instead, use a primitive coordinate
u(P)=x(P)+lambda y(P) and eliminate x from:
                                                            psi_5(x)=0
                                             (T-x)^2 - lambda^2(x^3 + A x + B) = 0

The resultant is generically degree 24 and has roots indexed by the 24 nonzero torsion points. Complex

common pair. This lane is valuable only if exceptional transitive images intersect live targets.

13. Attack 8: fixed-cubic-resolvent A4/S4 quartics over sextic bases
Let F/Q be sextic. Choose a cubic extension C/F. Quartic fields over F with local group A4 or S4 and cubic
resolvent C correspond to quadratic extensions of C satisfying a trivial-norm condition. This correspondence is
explicit and includes prescribed-signature refinements.
The resulting absolute degree is 4*6=24. Each real place of F can contribute 0, 2, or 4 real roots, providing
considerably broader even-signature control than the cyclic and dihedral fibers.
This lane is conditional because RouteA already used several quartic-over-sextic constructions. It counts as new
only if an exact action-level audit shows that the fixed-resolvent A4/S4 structures are absent from the historical
recipe and action catalogs. Otherwise it is rebranding and should be rejected.
